DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Standard spacesuits are heavy, rigid, and restrict movement. Astro Apparel Labs was founded on a simple question: Can we construct planetary exploration gear that feels like a normal windbreaker but delivers the isolation protection of an orbital capsule?

By blending advanced carbon aerogels, graphene fabrications, and active carbon nanotube lattices, we produce smart winter wear capable of withstanding absolute zero atmospheres without restricting flexibility.
